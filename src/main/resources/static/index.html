Sacred Heritage

Niloor Nimbekka Devi Temple

Kalaburagi, Karnataka

About the temple

Niloor Nimbekka Devi Temple is a revered local shrine dedicated to Goddess Nimbekka, a form of Shakti worshipped with deep faith by devotees in and around Niloor, Afzalpur taluk, Kalaburagi (Gulbarga) district. The temple stands as an important spiritual center for the surrounding villages and families who visit for blessings, peace, and protection.

Temple importance

The temple is known for its devotional atmosphere, strong local tradition, and regular worship practices. Devotees often visit during festival days, special poojas, and auspicious occasions to seek divine grace and offer prayers for health, prosperity, and family well-being.

Location Niloor, Afzalpur, Kalaburagi (Gulbarga), Karnataka
Deity Goddess Nimbekka Devi
Festivals Navaratri, Shakti Pooja, local temple celebrations
Community Village devotion and spiritual service
